1 · the observation

Every Attestcoin query proves a run of Ethereum block roots — then lets them go.

A readability query carries a continuity proof: an array of transaction Merkle roots chained by digest up to a stored attestation. 0x0FD2 verifies the whole chain, so acceptance binds every root in it.

Then the call returns and the roots are dropped. The next question about the same block pays for the same proof again.

This is not a flaw. Verifying is not the same job as remembering. It is the only part worth changing.

3 · Attestcoin depth

All three precompiles, used for what each one is for.

0x0FD2 · BlockProver

Write once. verifyAndEmit notarises a height and the archive retains the full continuity array it bound. Every later verify is a Merkle path against stored state.

0x0FD3 · ChainInfo

Self-repair. Checkpoints every 1,000 blocks at any depth. Roots computed from public receipts, the digest chain walked from a checkpoint, the precompile asked to reject it — it does not. The archive extends with the hosted prover switched off.

0x0FD4 · AttestorStash

The ceiling. The desk may not lend more than the attestor quorum behind the source chain has bonded, read live on every decision. 4 attestors × 100 CTC today.

Receipt status is checked before any log is trusted. No state proof is claimed. No absence is called a proof. Independence: the home page verifies a second transaction with 0x0FD2 deleted for the call — and the control, with the mirror deleted, fails — on every load.

7 · negatives are economic, and the hunt is a job

“Never liquidated” cannot be proven. It can be bonded, hunted, and burned.

/hunt lists every open bounty with what it pays, the measured gas to refute it (448k–4.0M) and the expected value. The board promises at least four documented lies open at all times; a replenisher keeps it so; a gate goes red if it slips.

The leaderboard is scored by tCTC burned — the only number you cannot farm by refuting yourself. Every name on it today is the house, and it says so.

Cover: an underwriter locks a payout on an open claim, a buyer pays a premium, and the claim’s own outcome decides who collects. No oracle — the hunt is the oracle. Run once with real money: bought for 0.05, the hunt refuted the claim, 1.0 tCTC collected. Four receipts.

Loans are sized to what a liar could not recover: ten times enforceableLoss, binding to the wei on a real Aave borrower.

The desk pays a Creditcoin key that has signed for its Ethereum address.

borrow underwrites msg.sender and takes no subject — a refusal must not be one parameter away from being washed. SubjectBinding closes the cost of that rule: an Ethereum address signs a transaction naming a Creditcoin key, proven against a held root, and the desk underwrites what that key speaks for. Replay-safe by Ethereum height. One record gets one loan however many keys its owner rotates through.

10 · what is not claimed

The honesty machine runs in public, and it says these out loud.

  • No vetted stranger. Both consumer contracts are the same person; every name on the leaderboard is the house; the only borrower paid is a house wallet. The site says so wherever a stranger would matter.
  • ~110 days, not 180. The deepening runs with the prover off at a measured rate; the run-unbroken gate will say when it gets there.
  • Nobody has bound an Ethereum address — it needs an Ethereum wallet with gas, and the binding contract has a proof pipeline tested to the last step.
  • Cover is on open claims, because a standing claim cannot be refuted and the registry’s rule won over the plan.
  • The Korean is machine-assisted and unreviewed. The hire-purchase corridor is not built.

CLAIMS.md grades every statement by its evidence and is generated from chain. /api/gates answers 503 the moment a promise breaks.
